QuizzyBug Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24-11-2026

QuizzyBug ("the app") is a learning app for children made by Anushya SR ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. In short: the app collects no personal information, and nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. QuizzyBug does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. That includes names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers and advertising IDs.

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • The app has no internet access. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • There are no links to websites or other apps.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a small amount of information only on your device:

  • Quiz results (scores, answers, stars and streaks), so children and grown-ups can see progress.
  • Settings, such as number ranges for math practice and the chosen difficulty level.

This information never leaves the device through the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved progress in your own Google account backup, so it can come back if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is done by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by QuizzyBug, and we cannot see it. You can turn backups off in your device settings at any time.

Children's privacy

QuizzyBug is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is no children's information for us to access, share or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. We will never change the app to collect children's personal information.
