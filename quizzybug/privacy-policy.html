QuizzyBug Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

QuizzyBug ("the app") is a learning app for children developed and published by Anushya SR ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. In short: the app collects no personal information, and nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. QuizzyBug does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. That includes names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, and advertising IDs.

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • The app has no internet access. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot transmit any data to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services.
  • QuizzyBug does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • There are no external links to websites or other apps.

What stays on your device

To function, the app saves a small amount of information only locally on your device:

  • Quiz results (scores, answers, stars, and streaks), so children and grown-ups can see progress.
  • App settings, such as number ranges for math practice and the chosen difficulty level.

This information never leaves your device through the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Device backups

If you have enabled Android's backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's locally saved progress in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new device. This backup is handled automatically by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by QuizzyBug, and we have no access to it. You can disable backups in your device settings at any time.

Children's privacy

QuizzyBug is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is no children's data on any server for us to access, share, or delete.

Parents and guardians can review or clear all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app, or by contacting us with any privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. We will never update the app to collect children's personal information.
